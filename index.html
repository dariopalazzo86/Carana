Group worksheet

Submit your group’s work

Submit when your group has finished. The worksheet locks for everyone in the group; staff can reopen it.

Incoming messages

Messages from the HOPC, the SMT and the JOC arrive here during the exercise. Respond to each, then update your plan.

Other groups

Read-only view of every group’s worksheet, for presentations and review.

Joint result

When the plan is complete, any group can submit the joint result on behalf of everyone. It locks the joint plan; staff can reopen it.

Library

Scenario documents for all CARANA exercises. They open in a new tab.

Add documents

Each file is stored in the database in pieces and is visible only to signed-in users. Up to about 20 MB per file.

Run the exercise

Choose the exercise, control the clock and the stage, adjust the scenario and send messages. Every group follows these settings live.

Groups

Create one login per group. During group work every group covers every topic; for the review and the joint result, give each group a topic. Logins stay the same across exercises; place people in groups in the Participants tab.

Progress and results

Rate each section from 0 (not done) to 5 (excellent). Totals count rated sections only. The joint plan is rated once and shown for every group.

Exercises

The three CARANA exercises are built in. Add your own scenarios here: write the brief, design the worksheet, set rating criteria and link the scenario PDF from the Library. New exercises appear in Run, Groups and Participants straight away.
