Final assessment

At the end of the course each group finalizes one scenario, starting from its own worksheet, and presents it to the SMT. Together, the groups’ final products form the final joint assessment.

Groups

Create one login per group (for example a shared group laptop). Participants with a personal login work in their group automatically. Place people in groups in Participants; assign final scenarios in Final assessment.

Participants

Register everyone on the course, give them a personal login, and place them in groups. Each participant belongs to one group for the whole course. With a personal login a participant can use the preparation course and the Library before the course, and during the course works in their group automatically.

Scenarios

The scenarios of the CARANA exercise. Colocation, FPU and RBB are built in; add your own here: write the brief, design the worksheet, set rating criteria and link the scenario PDF from the Library. New scenarios appear in Run, Groups and Participants straight away.

Reading log

Who opened which document in the Library, and when. Participants with a personal login are recorded automatically; people using a shared group login choose their name first. Admin openings aren’t recorded.

Admin and settings

Admins are the Directors, facilitators and mentors of the course; they all have the same rights. Add colleagues here: they sign in with their email and the password shown once.
