## Chapter 1: Carana at a glance

**Q3. About 40% of Carana’s adult population can read and write.** _(True or false)_

Explanation: Adult literacy is about 40%, and only 20% among young people under 18.

## Chapter 3: People and culture
**Q1. Where do the Kori mainly live?** _(One answer)_

- In the South, where they are the majority in Leppko
- ✅ In the West, where they dominate Tereni and Koloni
- In the East and the centre
- Along the northern coast

Hint: See “Ethnic Distribution”.  
Explanation: The Kori (about 20%) live in the West. The Falin live mainly in the East and centre; the Tatsi in the South (Leppko).

**Q2. Which statement about religion in Carana is correct?** _(One answer)_

- ✅ About 75% are Christian; Islam is practised by over 20%, mainly in the South and West
- Religious freedom is not provided for in the constitution
- Islam is practised mainly in Galasi

Hint: See “Religion”.  
Explanation: About 75% are Christian; Muslims (mostly Sunni) live mainly in the South and West. The suspended 2013 constitution provides for freedom of religion.

**Q3. Carana’s national borders match the ethnic distribution of the region.** _(True or false)_

- True
- ✅ False

Hint: Read the last paragraph of “Ethnic Distribution”.  
Explanation: The borders date from colonial times: Falin, Kori and Tatsi also form large parts of the populations of Sumora, Katasi and Rimosa.

**Q2. What has Carana’s political system been in practice since 2013?** _(One answer)_

## Chapter 12: Humanitarian situation, explosives and civil society
**Q1. Where do most of the roughly 700,000 IDPs live?** _(One answer)_

- In camps along the Katasi border
- ✅ In camps and shantytowns around the harbour cities, mostly around Galasi
- In Camp Lora
- In Sumora

Hint: Re-read the first paragraph of this chapter.  
Explanation: Most IDPs live in makeshift camps around the harbour cities, the largest part around Galasi. Camp Lora shelters about 30,000 Elassi refugees from Rimosa.

**Q2. Which statement about landmines in Carana is correct?** _(One answer)_

- Only the rebels used mines, and all minefields are recorded
- ✅ All sides used mines, and their locations are mostly unknown
- Carana ratified the Mine Ban Treaty in 2021
- Carana is a known exporter of anti-personnel mines

Hint: See “Explosives”.  
Explanation: Mines were used by all sides as a means of terror and deterrence, without documented obstacles. Carana signed but never ratified the treaty.

**Q3. Civil society in Carana is strong, with several influential groups outside the PDC party structure.** _(True or false)_

- True
- ✅ False

Hint: See “Civil society organizations”.  
Explanation: Civil society is weak; women’s and human rights organizations are only starting to form, while religious institutions and traditional chiefdoms have some influence.
